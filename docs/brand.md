# Brand & Visual Identity — IT Community of Uzbekistan

Pairs with `layout.md`. That file fixes **structure**; this one fixes **identity**. Everything else is yours.

**Be creative. Push the visuals.** Constraints below are the guardrails, not the design. Plain flat green is a failure state — make it richer than that.

## Who we are
IT Community of Uzbekistan — a community of people working in and passionate about IT/tech. The feeling: modern, energetic, human, a little bold. Tech with people at the center, not cold corporate.

## Color identity (non-negotiable)
- **Signature green** — vivid grass/lime green (~`#6CBE45`). The brand's voice. Use it with intent, not everywhere.
- **Deep green** — a darker companion (~`#2E9E33`) for gradients, depth, layering.
- **Ink** — near-black charcoal (~`#1E1E1E`) as the dominant canvas.
- **White** — surfaces and text on dark.

Rules: high contrast always. Green earns attention — don't dilute it across whole screens. Build depth by **layering the two greens** and green-on-dark, never one flat fill.

## Signature motif — use it, don't ignore it
The logo encodes a **node-and-branch / circuit** figure (dots connected by lines, a person/growth silhouette inside an organic rounded shield). That network-node language is the brand's fingerprint. Carry it through the UI as a recurring thread — subtle, not loud.

## Shape language (this is where you get creative)
We love **abstract, unique geometry that makes the interface quietly distinctive** — better than plain green blocks:
- Organic rounded-shield / leaf silhouettes (from the mark).
- **Hexagons**, diamonds/rhombus tessellations, faceted clusters.
- Network/constellation textures — connected dots drifting in backgrounds.
- Use these as **accents, edges, dividers, and subtle background texture** — never so heavy they fight the content. The goal: a screen that feels unmistakably *ours* on a half-second glance, without shouting.

## Type
Bold, geometric sans. Headings heavy and confident, often uppercase. Strong weight contrast between headings and body.

## Surfaces
Dark charcoal canvas, white content cards with soft rounded corners and gentle elevation. Green as the accent that ties it together — buttons, highlights, active states, key edges.

---
**The test:** keep the color identity and node/shape motif consistent, then make it feel crafted and distinctive — subtle, modern, clearly IT Community. Surprise me within these rails.
